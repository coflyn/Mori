Three taps. Done.

No setup, no accounts. Copy a link and Mori handles the rest.

  1. 1

    Copy the link

    Copy any video, photo, or music URL from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Spotify, Reddit, TeraBox, or any of the 16 supported platforms.

  2. 2

    Open Mori

    Mori detects the link from your clipboard the moment you open the app. On Android, tap Share in any app and pick Mori directly without switching screens.

  3. 3

    Pick quality and save

    Choose HD video, MP3 audio, or full photo gallery. Files go straight to your gallery or music folder, organized and complete.

Questions

There is no catch. Mori is free, open source (GPL-3.0), and runs entirely on your device. No ads, no analytics, no subscription. If you want to support development, there is a donation link.

Yes. Mori uses OTA (Over-The-Air) hot-patching to push scraper updates in the background without requiring you to reinstall the app. When a platform changes its API, the fix arrives silently.

Files go to standard system folders (Movies/Mori, Music/Mori, Pictures/Mori) by default. You can pick any custom folder, including SD cards, from Settings. Files only appear in your gallery once the download is 100% complete.

Mori for iOS ships as a .ipa file and requires sideloading via AltStore or TrollStore. It is not available on the App Store. The Installation Guide covers the full process step by step.

Yes. Full build instructions for all platforms are in CONTRIBUTING.md. The mobile app is built with Vanilla JS and Capacitor, with a companion Node.js CLI for desktop.

If you represent a platform or website and would like your service removed, modified, or have concerns regarding scraping, please open an issue on our GitHub Issues. Requests are reviewed and handled promptly.
